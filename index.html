About DSE

DSE (Data Science Enterprise) builds AI-native software for maritime and industrial operations.

Being founded by maritime expert, DSE combines deep frontline industry knowledge with expertise in software engineering, data science, and applied AI systems.

We focus on high-impact operational domains where legacy tools and generic software fail to meet real-world requirements — particularly in inspection workflows, compliance, reporting, and decision support.

Our mission is to digitize and optimize complex industrial processes using practical, reliable AI systems built on real operational experience.
